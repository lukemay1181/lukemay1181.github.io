01 / About me

A mathematical foundation. A practical mindset.

My work sits at the intersection of mathematics, computation, and data-informed decision making. Through graduate research and collaborative projects, I've used mathematical and computational models to explore how complex systems respond to changing conditions.

I enjoy taking abstract problems, breaking them into manageable pieces, and communicating the results clearly—whether that's through a simulation, technical report, or teaching someone a new concept.
